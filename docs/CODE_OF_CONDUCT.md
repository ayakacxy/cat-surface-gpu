# Community Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

All participants in this project's issues, discussions, and pull requests are expected to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and make room for different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's information without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized comments, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. These expectations apply regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately using the contact information on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/ayakacxy). Do not post personal details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation, depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
